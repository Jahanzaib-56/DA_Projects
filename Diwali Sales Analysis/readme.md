## 🔍 Project Overview

This project explores a real-world **Diwali Sales dataset** to uncover hidden trends, patterns, and customer behavior during the festive season. The goal is to generate **business insights** that can help improve marketing strategies and customer targeting.

📁 Dataset: `Diwali Sales.csv`  
📊 Tools: `Pandas`, `Matplotlib`, `Seaborn`, `Jupyter Notebook`

---

## ✨ Key Insights

Here’s what we uncovered from the data:

- 🔹 **Gender Trends**: Identified which gender spends more on average.
- 🔹 **Top Performing States**: Ranked states by sales volume and revenue.
- 🔹 **Product Category Analysis**: Determined most popular product categories.
- 🔹 **Age Group Preferences**: Found top age segments with highest engagement.
- 🔹 **Occupation & Marital Status Impact**: Tracked purchasing behavior based on job and relationship status.

> These insights are crucial for businesses looking to optimize advertising and product placement during festival campaigns.
